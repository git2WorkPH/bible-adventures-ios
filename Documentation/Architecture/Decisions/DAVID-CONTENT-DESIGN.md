# David and Goliath content design

Date: 2026-10-04. Status: APPROVED within PA-004-T04 definition scope and owner authorization for necessary in-scope decisions. Story implementation remains proposed. Owner explicitly selected David and Goliath.

Use 1 Samuel 17 as the story's source; follow the existing audience, GOD-centered reflection and Scripture Integrity Policy. Read the official ESV chapter during requirements review. Use narrator interpretation and source links for the initial proposed content. No new exact quotation is embedded by this design.

Use the existing quiz lifecycle and a configured pair-matching provisions activity. Three item categories are a learning aid; matching pairs do not assert Biblical quantities. Retain the historical outcome in brief, non-graphic narration. Do not build combat, weapon aiming, reaction timing or scoring that implies GOD's action depends on player skill. Do not turn the story into a promise that personal faith guarantees success in every situation.

Foundation dependencies: add First Samuel to BibleBook/reference validation; generalize the typed matching dispatch to supply per-story configuration; expose reviewed catalogue selection in Home with a runtime for the chosen story. Preserve per-story progress and existing noah-2 compatibility. Speaker expansion is unnecessary if initial narration remains narrator-only. New mechanics, dynamic code from JSON, save-slot migration and audio are unnecessary for this story.

Review: source references, questions and age-appropriate presentation are traced in DAVID-REQUIREMENTS.md. All final copy still requires PA-005's content audit, separate from this design review.
