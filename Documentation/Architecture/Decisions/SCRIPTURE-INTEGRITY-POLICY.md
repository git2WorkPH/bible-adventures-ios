# Scripture Integrity Policy

Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-09-04
Authority: Project owner decision recorded through FND-BASE-T02

## Scope

This policy applies to all present and future Bible Adventure story content, including on-screen dialogue, Scripture screens, questions, feedback, reflection, audio, and gameplay descriptions.

## 1. Scripture source and quotation rule

- The English Standard Version (ESV) is the approved source for direct Bible quotations.
- A direct quotation must reproduce the approved ESV text exactly. It must not be paraphrased, edited, combined with interpretation, or have source artifacts such as footnote markers inserted into its displayed text.
- Every displayed quotation must include its Bible book, chapter, and verse or verse range, plus the label `(ESV)`.
- The app must provide the applicable ESV copyright/permissions attribution and stay within Crossway's current digital-use permissions or obtain written permission before publishing quoted text. This policy does not itself grant a license.

## 2. Required content labels

| Content type | Required label | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Direct Bible text | `Scripture — [reference] (ESV)` | Contains only exact ESV quotation. |
| Explanation beyond the direct text | `Interpretation` | Must identify its supporting Scripture reference and must not be represented as a direct quotation or asserted as an explicit Biblical fact when the text is uncertain. |
| Interactive or visual invention | `Game activity` | May support learning but must not claim that its mechanics, visuals, dialogue, timing, or connective details are recorded in Scripture. |

## 3. Gameplay serves Scripture

Gameplay must be based on the relevant Scripture and must not alter Scripture's meaning. It may prompt players to read, recall, or apply a passage, but it must not replace the passage, create a Biblical claim, or present an interpretation as Scripture.

## 4. Noah ark wood terminology

- Direct quotation of Genesis 6:14 uses `gopher wood` when presenting ESV text.
- `Cypress wood` may appear only in an `Interpretation` label that makes clear it is an interpretation, not the text of Genesis 6:14.
- A question, feedback message, or game success condition must not treat `cypress wood` as an unqualified Biblical fact.

## 5. Implementation and review obligations

- Existing Noah content is prototype material and is not made compliant by this policy alone.
- Approved content/repository work must remove source-formatting artifacts, attach references, display the required labels, and assess ESV permissions/attribution before release.
- Each future story must be reviewed against this policy before it is verified.

## Decision rationale

The policy implements the project owner's decisions: ESV; exact text with chapter-and-verse citation; distinct `Scripture`, `Interpretation`, and `Game activity` labels; and `gopher wood` with `cypress` only as labelled interpretation.
