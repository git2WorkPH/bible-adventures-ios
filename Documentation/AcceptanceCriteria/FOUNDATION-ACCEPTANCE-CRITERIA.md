# Bible Adventure — Foundation Acceptance Criteria

## Global Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Foundation systems are reusable by multiple stories.
- [ ] Story-specific content is not hard-coded into reusable engines.
- [ ] Story progression is controlled by a central source of truth.
- [ ] Scripture references can be attached to relevant content.
- [ ] Questions support randomized answer order.
- [ ] Mini-games return results to the Story Engine.
- [ ] Failed activities do not corrupt story progression.
- [ ] Story completion can trigger reflection.
- [ ] Progress can be saved and restored.
- [ ] Foundation systems have automated tests where appropriate.
- [ ] iPhone and iPad layouts remain functional.
- [ ] Accessibility requirements are respected.

## Spiritual Purpose Acceptance

- [ ] Gameplay does not replace Scripture.
- [ ] Relevant Biblical passages are identified.
- [ ] Players are encouraged to read Scripture.
- [ ] Biblical facts are not fabricated.
- [ ] Gameplay inventions are not represented as Biblical facts.
- [ ] Story experiences preserve a GOD-centered purpose.
