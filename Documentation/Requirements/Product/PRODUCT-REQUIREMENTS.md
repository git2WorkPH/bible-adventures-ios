# Bible Adventure — Product Requirements

**Project:** Bible Adventure: Journey to Understanding Who GOD Is  
**Version:** 1.0 Draft  
**Status:** Requirements Definition

## Mission

Bible Adventure exists to encourage children and other players to genuinely read the Bible with the intention of seeking GOD.

> **The game is not the destination. The Bible is the destination.**

The game should encourage players to:
- become curious about Scripture
- read the actual Biblical passages
- understand what Scripture says
- reflect on what they have read
- seek GOD
- continue exploring Scripture beyond the game

## Product Principles

1. Scripture First
2. GOD-Centered
3. Biblically Responsible
4. Gameplay Serves the Message
5. Encourage Bible Reading
6. Age Appropriate
7. Fun Without Compromising the Message

## Core Product Requirements

### PRD-001 — Scripture-Centered Experience
The game must connect story experiences to relevant Scripture references and encourage players to read the Biblical source.

### PRD-002 — GOD-Centered Experience
Stories and gameplay should ultimately point the player toward understanding and seeking GOD.

### PRD-003 — Story-Based Gameplay
The game must present Biblical stories through interactive dialogue, objectives, questions, puzzles, mini-games, and reflection.

### PRD-004 — Reusable Foundation
Common gameplay capabilities must be implemented once in the foundation and reused by individual stories.

### PRD-005 — iPhone and iPad Support
The game must support iPhone and iPad with responsive SwiftUI interfaces.

### PRD-006 — Reflection
Completed story experiences should provide an opportunity for the player to reflect on what they learned and how it connects to Scripture.

## Product Success

The primary success criterion is not simply whether the player completes a game.

The stronger success criterion is:

> Does the game encourage the player to open the Bible, read Scripture, seek GOD, and continue that journey beyond the game?
