# Scripture Integrity Requirement

ID: FND-027
Status: Proposed

## Purpose

Ensure Bible Adventure remains faithful to Scripture while allowing interactive, age-appropriate gameplay.

## Requirement

The application must clearly distinguish Biblical Scripture, interpretation, and gameplay-created representation.

### Scripture

Content presented as Scripture must identify its Biblical reference and use an approved source/translation according to the project policy.

### Interpretation

Interpretations or explanations not explicit in the Biblical text must be presented as interpretation rather than direct Scripture.

### Gameplay representation

Visuals, puzzles, mechanics, and connective details may support the story, but must not alter Scripture's meaning or be presented as Biblical fact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Scripture source/translation policy approved.
- [ ] Scripture quotations trace to a Biblical reference.
- [ ] Interpretation is distinguishable from quoted Scripture.
- [ ] Gameplay representation is distinguishable from Scripture.
- [ ] Uncertain Biblical details are not silently converted into facts.
- [ ] Noah `gopher wood` / `cypress wood` treatment is explicitly decided.
- [ ] Source/formatting artifacts are removed from displayed Scripture.
- [ ] Future stories follow the same policy.

## Product intent

The game should encourage players to return to Scripture, read it themselves, and seek GOD through the Biblical text.
