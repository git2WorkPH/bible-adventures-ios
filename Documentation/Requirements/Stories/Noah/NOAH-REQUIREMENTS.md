# Bible Adventure — Noah Story Requirements

**Story:** Noah  
**Biblical Source:** Genesis 6–9  
**Status:** Requirements Definition

## Story Purpose

The Noah story should help players engage with the Biblical account of Noah, understand what Scripture records, encounter the story through interactive gameplay, and be encouraged to read Genesis 6–9 themselves.

## NOAH-001 — Story Flow
Define the complete Noah story from introduction through covenant and reflection.

## NOAH-002 — Wood Selection
Create an interactive activity based on the Biblical instruction concerning the ark's construction materials.

## NOAH-003 — Ark Measurement
Create a measurement activity based on the dimensions given for the ark.

## NOAH-004 — Ark Blueprint
Allow the player to understand and arrange the ark's required structure.

## NOAH-005 — Build the Ark
Provide a challenging puzzle-based construction activity using the foundation puzzle framework.

## NOAH-006 — Gather Food
Provide an interactive activity for preparing food for the people and animals.

## NOAH-007 — Gather Animals
Provide an interactive activity for identifying and preparing the animals required by the story.

## NOAH-008 — Enter the Ark
Provide an interactive story moment in which the player prepares for and enters the ark.

## NOAH-009 — Flood
Present the flood as part of the story progression while maintaining the distinction between Scripture and gameplay presentation.

## NOAH-010 — Dove
Provide an interactive activity around Noah sending the dove.

## NOAH-011 — Covenant
Provide an interactive covenant experience including the rainbow.

## NOAH-012 — Reflection
Provide a Scripture-connected reflection after the story.

## Story Requirements

Every Noah component must define:
- Biblical source
- purpose
- player learning objective
- gameplay mechanic
- Scripture reference
- dialogue
- success/failure behavior
- acceptance criteria
- reflection opportunity

## Biblical Accuracy Rule

Gameplay mechanics may simplify or dramatize interaction for game purposes, but gameplay-created details must not be presented as if they were explicitly stated by Scripture.
