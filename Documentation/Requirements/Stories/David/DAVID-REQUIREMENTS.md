## Questions and reflection contract

Proposed questions, all labelled Interpretation with source hints and reviewed feedback:

- Who did David say had delivered him before? Correct: the LORD; distractors Saul/Goliath; 17:37.
- Why did David put off the armor? Correct: he had not tested it; distractors it was missing/it belonged to Goliath; 17:39. Do not state merely that it was too large.
- How many smooth stones did David choose? Correct: five; distractors one/ten; 17:40.
- What does David say about how the LORD saves? Correct: not with sword and spear; distractors the strongest armor/the biggest army; 17:47. This is interpretation of the verse, not a displayed quotation.

Reflection draft (Interpretation): “Read 1 Samuel 17:45–47. What does David's trust teach you about GOD? What would you like to ask or explore as you read the chapter?” Do not promise victory over every difficulty or treat confidence, game success or reflection completion as proof of faith.
